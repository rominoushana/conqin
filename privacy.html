Privacy Policy

Conqin, the Assyrian card game, by ROLabs · Last updated 16 November 2026

In short: games against the computer, Story Mode and the tutorial keep everything on your phone. If you play online, we store only what online play needs: the name you choose, an account ID, your friends and your game results. No ads, no tracking, and we never sell your data.

Playing offline

Quick Games, Story Mode, the tutorial, your saved games, your progress and your settings are stored only on your device. Nothing about them is sent to us.

Playing online

To play online you sign in, either as a guest (you just choose a name) or with Sign in with Apple on iPhone. We then store:

WhatWhy
Your display nameShown to other players at your table, to your friends and on leaderboards.
An account ID (random for guests; for Sign in with Apple, the anonymous ID Apple gives us)To keep your account, friends and stats together.
Your friend code, friends and friend requestsSo you can add friends and invite them to a table.
Tables you join and the moves played thereOnline games are stored as a list of moves so every player's phone shows the same table, and so you can rejoin.
Games played and wonFor your stats and the leaderboards.
Invites to tablesSo a friend's invite reaches you.

Table chat messages and emoji reactions are sent live to the other players at your table and are not stored. We don't ask Apple for your email address. We don't collect your location, contacts, photos or device identifiers.

Who processes it

Online data is stored with Supabase, our hosting provider, which processes it only to run Conqin. It's encrypted in transit (HTTPS). We don't use advertising, analytics or tracking services, and we don't sell or share your data.

How long we keep it

Until you delete your account. When you do, your profile, friend code, friends, invites, stats and results are deleted. Moves you made in games other people were playing stay in those games (so their games can still be replayed) but are no longer linked to you.

Deleting your account

In the app: home screen → Account (or Play online) → Delete Account. It happens immediately. You can also ask us to delete it — see Delete your Conqin account.

Children

Conqin isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from them. If you think a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
