Delete your Conqin account

Conqin, the Assyrian card game, by ROLabs

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open Conqin.
  2. On the home screen, tap Account next to “Signed in as…”.
  3. Tap Delete Account, then Delete to confirm.

Without the app

Email ROLabs33@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Conqin account” and include your display name and friend code (shown under your name in the app). We'll delete the account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted

  • Your display name, account ID and friend code
  • Your friends, friend requests and table invites
  • Your stats, game results and leaderboard entries

What stays

  • Moves you made in games other people were playing stay in those games so they can still be replayed, but they're no longer linked to you.
  • Games, progress and settings saved on your own phone stay there until you delete the app.
